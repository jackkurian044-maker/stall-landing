Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how STall ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and app.

Information we collect

  • Account information you provide, such as name, email, and phone number.
  • Business listing details submitted by vendors, including location and photos.
  • Usage data such as searches performed and pages viewed, to improve the service.
  • Approximate or precise location, only when you choose to share it for nearby search.

How we use information

  • To operate and improve search, listings, and business dashboards.
  • To communicate with you about your account or listing.
  • To process payments for Premium subscriptions.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information with service providers (such as payment processors and mapping services) only as needed to operate STall.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.
