Help Center

Support for customers and business owners using STall.

For customers

Have a question about finding or contacting a business on STall?

  • Use the search bar or "Use Current Location" on the homepage to find nearby businesses.
  • Tap a listing to see hours, contact details, reviews, and directions.
  • Use the Call, WhatsApp, or Website buttons on a listing to reach a business directly.

For business owners

Managing your listing on STall?

  • Register your business to appear in local search results.
  • Update your hours, photos, and services from your vendor dashboard.
  • Upgrade to Premium for AI-powered review responses and featured placement.

Still need help?

Reach us any time — see our Contact page or email us and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.
