# About Us ## Discover. Connect. That's All. STall was built on a simple idea: the best businesses in your life aren't always the biggest ones — they're the flower shop around the corner, the salon your friend swears by, the café where the barista already knows your order. They're right there in your neighbourhood, but they're often the hardest to actually *find*. That's the gap STall was built to close. ## Our Story STall started as a one-person build — designed, developed, and shipped from the ground up on Google Cloud, from an idea into a real platform that verified vendors, onboards businesses through a claim-code process, and now helps neighbourhoods rediscover the businesses in their own backyard. It's still early days, and we're building this in public, one neighbourhood at a time. ## What We Do STall is a hyperlocal discovery platform that connects people with trusted, verified businesses nearby — restaurants, salons, grocers, cafés, pharmacies, and everything in between. No endless scrolling, no guesswork. Just search, explore, and connect — instantly, by call, chat, or map. ## Why We Exist - **For customers** — a faster, more trustworthy way to find what's around you, backed by real reviews and verified listings. - **For businesses** — visibility that doesn't require a big marketing budget. Get discovered by the people already looking for you. - **For neighbourhoods** — a platform that keeps local commerce local, one connection at a time. ## Our Promise Every business on STall is verified. Every listing is built to help you decide faster and connect instantly. We're not trying to be everything — just the fastest way to find what's *all* around you. **Discover. Connect. That's All.**
