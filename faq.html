Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about STall, business discovery, and our growing geographic coverage.

Is STall free to use for customers?

Yes. Searching for and contacting local businesses on STall is completely free.

How do I list my business?

Click Register Business and follow the short onboarding flow — store info, location, hours, photo, and account details.

Is there a cost for businesses?

Basic listings are free. A Premium plan is available for additional features like AI review responses and featured placement.

Which cities and states does STall cover?

STall is an India-wide local business discovery platform with current presence in Bengaluru, Delhi, Kerala and Tamil Nadu. Our discovery model is organized by State → City → Category → Business, so customers can discover participating businesses based on where they are.

Does STall cover Delhi?

Yes. STall has a presence in Delhi and supports location-based discovery of participating restaurants, salons, shops, services and other local businesses.

Does STall cover Kerala?

Yes. STall has a presence in Kerala and supports state, city and category-based discovery of participating local businesses.

Does STall cover Tamil Nadu?

Yes. STall has a presence in Tamil Nadu and supports state, city and category-based discovery of participating local businesses.

Does STall cover Bengaluru?

Yes. Bengaluru is one of STall's active Indian markets. STall is also expanding its coverage to additional cities across India.

Is STall available outside India?

STall is expanding internationally, with early business listings in Dubai. International city coverage will grow as more participating businesses are added.

How do I claim an existing listing?

If your business was added by an admin on your behalf, you'll receive a one-time claim code you can enter to take over editing.
