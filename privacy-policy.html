Privacy Policy

Last updated: [Date]

STall ("we," "us," "our") operates the STall platform, connecting customers with local businesses. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using STall, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information from users and registered businesses:

  • Name — to personalize your account and communications
  • Email address — for account access, verification, and support
  • Location — to show you nearby businesses and help businesses appear to customers nearby

We do not collect payment card details directly — payments for business subscriptions are processed by our payment partner, Razorpay, under their own privacy and security policies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Show relevant local businesses based on your location
  • Verify business listings during onboarding
  • Communicate with you about your account, bookings, or support requests
  • Improve and maintain the STall platform

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Businesses you contact — so they can respond to you
  • Service providers (e.g., Razorpay for billing, cloud hosting providers) who help us operate the platform
  • Legal authorities, if required by law

4. Your Choices

You can:

  • Update or correct your account information at any time
  • Disable location access through your device settings (this may limit nearby-search features)
  • Request deletion of your account and data by contacting us

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no online platform can guarantee complete security.

6. Children's Privacy

STall is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll post the updated version here with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Reach out through our Contact page →
